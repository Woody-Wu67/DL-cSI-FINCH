Pretrained Weights
==================

Due to GitHub's file size limit, the pretrained weight files are not included
in this repository.

To obtain the weights, please contact the author by email:

    doctorwhy@gdut.edu.cn

Please include the repository name and the model/experiment you need in your
request. The files will be sent as a download link.

After downloading, place the weight files in the following directory:

    ./weights/

Note: The weights are provided for academic and non-commercial research use
only.
